BENCHMARK PROTOCOL SPOT CHECK — 2026-10-09 r3
Read-only review of primary paper methodology. No new experiment was run.

ULF-Loc (https://arxiv.org/html/2605.04730v1, Sec. 4.1):
  7Scenes and 12Scenes use SfM pseudo-GT poses from Brachmann et al. (ICCV 2021);
  Cambridge uses Cambridge dataset GT. Their tables are literature comparisons.
  The relevant baseline 12Scenes appendix reports PNeRFLoc with Office 2 5a
  unreported, 11 other numerical cells, and published mean 2.8 / 1.08.
  Its generic caption says "mean over all 12 scenes"; for that one partial row,
  the displayed 11-cell average matches the reported mean to shown precision.
  Our T3 flags the partial coverage instead of silently calling it all 12.

SplitGS-Loc (https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07351v1, Sec. 4.1):
  Uses SfM-based pose annotations from Brachmann et al. (ICCV 2021) for
  7Scenes; evaluates five Cambridge scenes. SplitGS-Loc+ is a distinct
  iterative pose refinement configuration and remains a distinct table row.
  This paper does not report 12Scenes in the cited comparison.

GSVisLoc (https://arxiv.org/html/2508.18242v1, Sec. 4.1, Tables 1–2):
  7Scenes results use SfM annotations and original train/test split.
  Cambridge reports only King's College, Old Hospital, Shop Facade,
  St Mary's Church (four scenes; excludes Great Court).
  Our Cambridge table retains the four-scene mean marker.

STDLoc (https://arxiv.org/html/2503.19358v1, Sec. 4):
  Original reported Cambridge and 7Scenes rows were transcribed from STDLoc.
  STDLoc explicitly evaluates five Cambridge scenes and seven indoor scenes.
  We did not independently establish every underlying published competitor's
  exact pose-file version from STDLoc's table. Avoid claiming all 24/26/16
  published baselines share identical inputs/poses/training budgets.

On the Limits of Pseudo Ground Truth (Brachmann et al., ICCV 2021):
  https://github.com/tsattler/visloc_pseudo_gt_limitations
  Provides alternative SfM pGT for 7Scenes/12Scenes; poses under different
  references can change numerical rankings.

12Scenes PNeRFLoc missing-value cross-check:
  ULF-Loc Table A reproduces 11 per-scene entries and 2.8 / 1.08 mean.
  A separate SplatLoc study reports PNeRFLoc failed Office 2 5a
  (https://scispace.com/pdf/splatloc-3d-gaussian-splatting-based-visual-localization-for-tak5ch8emddh.pdf).
  The 11-scene interpretation is supported by displayed arithmetic, not a
  newly recomputed unrounded published metric. T3 flags the coverage mismatch.

Conclusion for the paper:
  Published benchmark tables (T1–T3) provide literature context under the
  reported methods and scene coverage. T4 remains the controlled local
  comparison with explicit mask/stage/native-cost settings. Do not call all
  literature numbers same-hardware or same-prior local reruns.
